# VaidyaAI — Hackathon 3-Minute Video Plan

> **Target:** 180 seconds | **Format:** Screen recording with voiceover | **Goal:** Demonstrate AI is LIVE, clinical workflow is real, human remains in control

---

## Segment 1: Problem + Product Introduction (0:00 — 0:20)

**Visual:** VaidyaAI login screen → Dashboard with live queue

**Voiceover Script:**
> "In India, a single outpatient doctor sees 60–80 patients a day. Clinical documentation, prescriptions, billing, and follow-ups consume more time than patient care itself. VaidyaAI is an AI-native clinical operating system that runs 7 autonomous agents — powered by Google Gemini 2.5 — to handle documentation, safety, billing, and patient engagement while the doctor focuses on medicine."

**Key Evidence Points:**
- Show real login and dashboard loading
- Point out "7 AI agents operational" status bar

---

## Segment 2: Patient Registration + Queue (0:20 — 0:45)

**Visual:** Walk-in modal → Patient registration → Queue update

**Voiceover Script:**
> "A walk-in patient arrives. Reception registers them with a phone number and chief complaint. AppointmentFlow, our first AI agent running on Gemini 2.5 Flash, triages WhatsApp bookings and manages the queue."

**Actions:**
1. Click "Walk-In" button
2. Enter patient details (phone, complaint, vitals)
3. Show patient appearing in queue
4. Show AgentStatusBar with AppointmentFlow activity

---

## Segment 3: Live Clinical Consultation + ClinicalScribe (0:45 — 1:30)

**Visual:** Consultation workspace → Ambient recording → SOAP note generation

**Voiceover Script:**
> "The doctor starts a consultation. Our ClinicalScribe agent captures the ambient conversation, runs Speech-to-Text diarization, anonymizes PHI, and sends the transcript to Gemini 2.5 Pro for structured SOAP note generation with ICD-10 clinical coding."

**Actions:**
1. Open consultation workspace
2. Start ambient recording (or use pre-recorded audio)
3. Show transcript appearing in real-time
4. Click "Generate SOAP" — show processing indicator
5. Show SOAP note populating (Subjective, Objective, Assessment, Plan)
6. **CRITICAL:** Point camera at the ClinicalScribe provenance banner showing:
   - "Generated By: ClinicalScribe (Agent 2)"
   - "Provider: Google Cloud Vertex AI"
   - "Model: gemini-2.5-pro"
   - "Region: asia-south1"
   - "Latency: XXms"
   - "Confidence: XX% (STT)"

---

## Segment 4: Gemini 2.5 Pro Provenance + SOAP + ICD-10 (1:30 — 1:55)

**Visual:** SOAP editor → ICD-10 codes → Medication list

**Voiceover Script:**
> "The AI-generated SOAP note includes structured ICD-10 diagnosis codes and medication extraction. The doctor can review and edit every field — AI drafts, human decides. Notice the model provenance: every AI action is traceable to the exact Gemini model, region, and timestamp."

**Actions:**
1. Show ICD-10 codes extracted (e.g., J06.9 - Acute Upper Respiratory Infection)
2. Show medication list extracted
3. Show doctor editing the Assessment field (demonstrate human-in-the-loop)
4. Highlight the allergy review gate: "Allergy status must be reviewed before prescription"

---

## Segment 5: PrescriptionSafe + Human Safety Review (1:55 — 2:20)

**Visual:** Safety panel → Drug interaction check → Override modal

**Voiceover Script:**
> "PrescriptionSafe, running on Gemini 2.5 Pro, performs autonomous drug-drug interaction analysis, allergy conflict detection, and dose safety verification. If the AI is unavailable, the system fails closed — it flags the prescription for mandatory manual review rather than assuming safety."

**Actions:**
1. Show SafetyFlagsPanel with green "Safe" or orange "Warning"
2. If warning exists, show the override modal requiring clinical rationale
3. Doctor approves the SOAP note → Click "Approve & Finalize"
4. Show status transition from "Draft" to "Approved"

---

## Segment 6: Billing + Operational AI (2:20 — 2:40)

**Visual:** Billing page → Invoice generation → Payment

**Voiceover Script:**
> "On approval, BillingPulse automatically generates a sequentially-numbered invoice with correct tax calculations and sends a Razorpay UPI payment link via WhatsApp. The daily P&L reconciles every rupee."

**Actions:**
1. Show invoice appearing in billing dashboard
2. Show Razorpay payment link generated
3. Show financial summary (collected, pending, collection rate)
4. Point to BillingSidebar showing BillingPulse agent decisions

---

## Segment 7: AI Workforce + Audit Evidence + Business Impact (2:40 — 3:00)

**Visual:** Settings → AI Agents tab → Audit Trail → Final statement

**Voiceover Script:**
> "Every AI decision is logged with full provenance — agent, model, latency, success/failure, and correlation IDs. 7 autonomous agents, 2 Gemini models, running live in production on Google Cloud Vertex AI. VaidyaAI doesn't just use AI as a feature — AI IS the business."

**Actions:**
1. Navigate to Settings → AI Agents tab
2. Show all 7 agents with their status, model, tasks, latency, success rate
3. Switch to Audit Trail tab — show live agent decision logs
4. Final shot: Dashboard overview with queue, agents, and clinical workflow all visible

---

## Production Tips

1. **Before recording:** Run at least 2-3 real consultations so agent logs and metrics are populated
2. **Audio:** Use a real (or realistic) doctor-patient conversation for the ambient scribe demo
3. **Speed:** Keep transitions fast — judges have seen hundreds of demos
4. **Evidence:** Linger on the provenance banner and agent dashboard — these prove AI is LIVE
5. **Don't:** Show slides, talk about future plans, or spend time on the login screen
